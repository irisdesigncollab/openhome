Life at home, a little lighter.

OpenHome is a personal assistant for the whole household. Ask it anything, hand off the little jobs, and let it keep track of what you’d rather not — from a speaker that fits right in.

Pre-order

Every home runs on a hundred little things.

The pasta timer. The permission slip due Friday.

The birthday you swore you wouldn’t forget.

OpenHome helps you keep up with all of it —

there when you call,

quiet when you don’t,

and a soft glow lets you know it’s on it.

The short version.

  • A personal assistant for everyone at home.

  • Timers, reminders, recipes, and answers. Just say the word.

  • Keeps track of the plans you mention, so you don’t have to.

Glow

You’ll always know when it’s listening.

A soft glow rises on top when it hears you, drifts while it’s working something out, and fades as soon as it’s done. When something’s waiting for you — a reminder, a finished task — a gentle light underneath lets you know, without saying a word.

  • A touch away. Tap the top to stop it mid-sentence, or rest your hand there to talk.

  • Speaks in color. Abilities can use the light too, like a slow countdown for the oven or a warm glow when the kids get home.

Sound

Sounds as good as it listens.

Put on a dinner playlist and it fills the room. Ask a question over the sizzle of the pan and it still catches every word. And when it answers, its voice comes through clear and natural, like someone right there in the kitchen with you.

  • Hears you from the hallway. It picks up your voice from across the room, even with music playing.

  • Soft to the touch. Wrapped in a woven fabric that lets the sound through and looks right at home on any shelf.

Inside

There’s a lot of thought in here.

Every piece inside has one simple job: to help you without getting in the way.

  1. Ears. Microphones that pick out your voice from across the room.

  2. A glow. Lights above and below that show how it’s doing.

  3. A voice. A speaker tuned for conversation first, and music right after.

  4. A deep breath. A hollow inside that gives the sound room to grow.

  5. A mind. A tiny computer that thinks with the AI you choose.

Then it all comes back together, and simply fits in.

Privacy

The perfect houseguest.

OpenHome knows when to listen and when to stay out of it. A soft light shows whenever it’s paying attention, one touch on top mutes it, and anything it remembers lives in a history you can look through, fix, or clear. You decide which AI it works with and what it’s allowed to see.

For developers

Open all the way down.

The platform behind OpenHome is open to builders: an open-source Voice SDK, docs, a DevKit, and a community that’s already building. Prototype something over a weekend, or build a product on top of it.

Start building ›

import { Ability } from "@openhome/sdk";

export default new Ability({
  name: "Plant care",
  when: "Did anyone water the plants?",
  answer: ({ memory }) => memory.last("watered the plants"),
});

Good questions.

What can OpenHome help with?

Everyday things around the house: answering questions, timers and reminders, music, the family calendar, and lights and other smart devices. Abilities add more.

Can everyone in the family use it?

Yes. Anyone at home can ask questions, play music, and set timers. [Personal features for each family member]

Is OpenHome always listening?

It listens for its wake word. Listening mode, which lets it pick up plans and reminders from conversation, stays off until you turn it on. A soft light shows whenever it’s paying attention, and you can mute it at any time.

How is it different from other smart speakers?

It’s built around your household, not around one company’s assistant. You choose the AI it uses, teach it new abilities, and can always see what it’s doing.
