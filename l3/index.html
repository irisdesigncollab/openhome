Inside OpenHome

Every part, in its place.

  1. 01Textured top

    Three layers in one surface: a frosted outer face with etched control symbols that catch the light, a transparent capacitive touch layer, and a diffuser of varying thickness that ripples the light beneath it.

    • Capacitive touch
    • Etched, illuminated controls
  2. 02Top light array

    A field of RGB LEDs sits beneath the diffuser, set back from the surface so the light spreads evenly, without hot spots. It brightens as OpenHome listens and follows your touch across the top.

    • RGB LEDs
    • Responsive lighting
  3. 03Passive radiators

    Two passive radiators sit on the side faces of the chassis, working with the driver to give the sound more depth than its size suggests.

    • 2 passive radiators
  4. 04Driver

    A single forward-facing driver, fed directly from the main board.

    • 1 driver
  5. 05Main board

    The main board mounts on the back of the internal chassis. With a gasket, it seals the acoustic chamber and keeps taller components tucked inside.

    • Sealed acoustic chamber
  6. 06Illuminated logomark

    A small front-facing light board brings the dotted OH mark to life through the fabric.

    • Front light
  7. 07Refractive base

    Textured polycarbonate, patterned near its top edge, bends light from a lower LED array into soft caustic patterns around the speaker. A rubberised foot keeps it planted.

    • 24 mm light base
    • Caustic light
    • Rubberised foot
