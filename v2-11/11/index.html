Design

Looks like it’s always lived here.

Soft fabric, rounded corners, and a gentle glow. OpenHome sits on the kitchen counter, the bookshelf, or the nightstand like it was made for the spot, and blends in until you need it.

Touch and light

A touch away.

Talk to it from across the room, or reach out and touch the top. Every gesture answers in light.

  • Rest your hand on the top to talk. It listens until you let go.

  • Stop it mid-sentence.

  • Dismiss a timer or a reminder. It stops ringing and marks it done.

  • Mute the microphones. It stops listening until you triple-tap again.

  • Away from you for louder, toward you for softer.
